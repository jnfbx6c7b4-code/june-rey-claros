About me

I turn scattered content into a system that compounds.

I'm June Rey Claros — a freelance social media manager, video editor and content strategist with 4+ years helping businesses, startups, agencies, coaches, real estate teams and e-commerce brands show up online with intention.

Most brands don't have a content problem — they have a consistency and strategy problem. I fix that end to end: research your audience, build the content pillars and calendar, produce and edit the assets, publish on schedule, then read the analytics and double down on whatever is actually driving saves, shares, leads and sales.

The result is a feed that looks like one brand, a posting rhythm you can rely on, and short-form video that stops the scroll in the first three seconds.

Process

A workflow built for consistency, not chaos.

Eight steps, repeated every month. You always know what's happening and what's next.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A short call to map your goals, offers, audience and what "winning" looks like in numbers.

  2. 02

    Research

    Competitor teardown, audience listening and a sweep of the formats currently working in your niche.

  3. 03

    Planning

    Content pillars, hook bank, posting cadence and a calendar approved before a single asset is made.

  4. 04

    Content Creation

    Scripts, captions, graphics and shot lists — produced in batches so quality stays even.

  5. 05

    Editing

    Cutting, captioning, sound design, colour and design polish until every asset is on-brand.

  6. 06

    Scheduling

    Everything queued to the best slots for your audience, with community replies handled daily.

  7. 07

    Analytics

    Reach, retention, saves, shares and conversions — one clear monthly report, no vanity metrics.

  8. 08

    Optimization

    Double down on what performed, cut what didn't, and feed the learnings into next month's plan.
